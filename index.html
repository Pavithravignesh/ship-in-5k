FAQ

Questions founders ask us

Still unsure? Bring your questions to a free discovery call.

What tech stack do you use?

Mostly React.js, Next.js and Node.js. If your project needs a different stack, we can work with that too.

How will you track the work?

We run the project on a Git project board that your team can access, and send you a progress update every four days on WhatsApp or email.

Are there any refunds if I don’t like the service?

We don’t offer refunds. You can cancel at any time, though, and you won’t owe the remaining balance.

Who will design the UI for the application?

We design the UI in Figma. If you already have designs, we’ll build from those.

Is there any work you won’t take?

We usually don’t take projects with heavy backends, extensive animations or several complex modules. If you’re not sure, book a call and we’ll talk it through.

What if our project can’t be done in 5 weeks for $5K?

We still take larger projects. After the discovery call we’ll share a timeline and pricing that fits the scope.

When will you start the work?

Work starts one week after we receive the advance payment.

What have you built before?

Past projects include an AI-based database monitoring system and an AI customer support platform, alongside the client products above.

Can we see previous projects and talk to past clients?

Yes. On a call we can show demos and connect you with founders and CXOs we’ve worked with.

How do we pay the $5K?

A $1K advance to begin, then $1K each week. That totals $5K across the five weeks.
